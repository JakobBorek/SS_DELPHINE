Refit

November 2024 – July 2025

An eighteen-month return to service

One of the most comprehensive refits in Delphine’s modern history, overseen by Maxime Berisset of VIENA MB as representative owner and project manager.

  1. 01 · Marseille

    Found and assessed

    Delphine was found in very poor condition in Marseille and fully dismantled by the shipyard.

  2. 02 · Eighteen months

    Rebuilt with purpose

    Hundreds of specialists, engineers, craftspeople and marine professionals addressed structural work, engineering, mechanical systems, electrical systems, navigation, safety, onboard services and finishes.

  3. 03 · Return to service

    Original intent retained

    Original details were preserved wherever possible and modern technology integrated discreetly. Her steam propulsion plant was extensively maintained and restored.

Sea trials successfully completed.
