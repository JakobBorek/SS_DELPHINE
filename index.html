The Golden Age Returns

Built in 1921 for the Dodge family, SS Delphine still sails under the original engines she was designed for.

The Yacht

Built in 1921 for the Dodge family, SS Delphine is the last operational privately owned steam yacht of her size.

Her original Horace Dodge quadruple expansion steam engines remain at the heart of a vessel restored for modern operation.

She accommodates twenty-six overnight guests across twelve cabins and is available for private charter and quayside events.

Private Charter

SS Delphine is available for private charter and for quayside events for more than 180 guests.

Pets are welcome; parties, events and night navigation are subject to prior approval by the captain and management.

Every departure is confirmed at least six hours in advance, giving the steam boilers time to reach pressure.

Rates are on request.
