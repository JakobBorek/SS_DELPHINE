The Refit

In November 2024, Maxime Berisset of VIENA MB was appointed as representative owner and project manager to lead the entire refit.

Found in very poor condition in Marseille, SS Delphine was dismantled from every corner before more than eighteen months of work were completed in July 2025.

The objective was to preserve her authenticity while meeting modern operational, technical and safety standards.

Inside the restoration

Hundreds of specialists, engineers, craftsmen and marine professionals worked on virtually every aspect of the vessel.

Her steam propulsion plant underwent extensive maintenance and restoration so she could continue to operate exactly as originally designed.

Major structural work, engineering upgrades, mechanical overhauls, electrical improvements, navigation systems, safety equipment and onboard services were addressed. All engines and major equipment, cosmetics, varnish, painting and carpets were refitted.

Original details were restored wherever possible while modern technology was discreetly integrated. Successful sea trials marked her return to service and demonstrated the performance of her propulsion systems.

Interiors and Amenities

The gym includes two Concept 2 exercise bikes, two rowing machines, a multi-gym, a full set of dumbbells, an adjustable weight bench and an ab crunch machine.

An eight-seat hammam, an eight-seat sauna, a two-seat hair salon and a twelve-seat Jacuzzi complete the wellness facilities.

Smart televisions and high quality audio run throughout, supported by movie and music libraries, satellite television, outdoor television and an outdoor sound system.

Smoking is not permitted inside SS Delphine.

Charter Details

Rates are on request. SS Delphine is available for private charter and for quayside events for more than 180 guests.

She is YET 12, ISM, ISPS and MLC compliant.

Pets are welcome. Smoking is not permitted inside the yacht.

Night navigation, parties and events are subject to prior approval by the captain and management. Departures must be confirmed at least six hours in advance to allow the steam boilers to reach pressure.
